How work is reviewed
## Manual review
The Agent Games team reviews each entry manually. These are the qualities reviewers consider:

### Technical substance
Show what you built and the decisions behind it.

### Clarity
Help someone unfamiliar with your project follow the work.

### zkVerify relevance
Explain where verification matters to your build.

### Originality
Share your own work and perspective.

Formats
## Make it yours

THREAD
A public thread with a clear technical point.

VIDEO
A demo, tutorial, or practical walkthrough.

ARTICLE / DEEP DIVE
A written article, guide, or deep dive about your build.

OTHER
A standalone post that doesn't fit the above — e.g. a LinkedIn or Reddit post.

Need a place to start?
## Content ideas
Not sure what to post? Pick an idea below, mix a few together, or go with your own.
Threads, videos, articles, and posts on X, Instagram, YouTube, or another public platform are all welcome.

- 01
Tell people Agent Games is coming: what the four tracks are and the prizes up for grabs — 400,000 VFY tokens total, with 100,000 VFY tokens per track.
- 02
Can an AI agent back up what it says? Pick one claim and show what would count as proof.
- 03
What should an agent check before trusting another agent? Make a simple checklist.
- 04
Imagine two agents working together. What would they need to show each other before moving ahead?
- 05
Pick a real job an AI agent could do, and show where zkVerify would help.
- 06
Show the agent you built: why you made it, how it works, and a quick demo.

To be eligible
Include #AgentGames in every entry. If you post on X, also tag @ZKVProtocol.
